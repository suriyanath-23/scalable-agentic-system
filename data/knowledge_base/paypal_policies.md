# PayPal operating policies

Sellers have 10 calendar days to respond to a dispute before automatic resolution in the buyer's favor. Claims can take up to 30 days to process.

Invoice fees are 2.9% plus $0.30 per invoice. Unpaid invoices expire automatically after 30 days. A seller should confirm the recipient, amount, currency, and terms before sending.

Refunds, payouts, dispute escalation, and subscription cancellation are financial or account-changing actions. They require authorization and an idempotency key in production.
